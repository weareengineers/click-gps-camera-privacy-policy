Privacy Policy — CLiCK GPS Camera

Effective date: October 4, 2026

This policy describes what CLiCK GPS Camera ("the app", "we") does and does not do with your data. It was written to match exactly what the app's code does — read it alongside play-store/DATA_SAFETY.md, which maps these same facts onto Google Play's Data Safety form.

The short version

CLiCK GPS Camera works almost entirely on your device. It does not have user accounts, does not run ads, and does not upload your photos, videos, or location to servers we operate — we don't operate any servers for this app. It does use Firebase Analytics to understand overall usage (see below) — that's the one exception to "stays on the device," and it's limited to app-usage data, not your photos, videos, or GPS coordinates. Separately, two Android/Google system services the app relies on to do its job (location lookup and address lookup) also involve data leaving the device, described below.

What the app accesses, and why

PermissionWhy the app asks for it
CameraTo show the live preview and capture photos/videos.
MicrophoneTo record audio as part of a video. Not used at any other time.
Location (fine/coarse)To read your GPS coordinates and stamp them into the photo/video pixels, and to look up a human-readable address for the same stamp (see "Address lookup" below).
Photos and videos / StorageTo save the photos and videos you capture into your device's own gallery, and to let you open an existing photo from your gallery to add a stamp to it.
NotificationsTo show the progress and result of video stamping, which can continue running in the background after you leave the app.

The app does not request background location access — location is only read while you're actively using the app (the camera screen is open, or a video is being stamped that you just recorded).

What happens to your photos, videos, and location data

  • Photos and videos are captured and processed entirely on your device. The GPS

stamp (coordinates, address, date/time) is drawn directly into the image/video pixels on-device.

  • The stamped result is saved to your device's own gallery (via Android's

MediaStore), exactly like a photo from your regular camera app. We never receive a copy.

  • Nothing is uploaded anywhere by this app. If you later share a photo or video

yourself (e.g. via a messaging app), that happens through the app you chose to share it with, under that app's own privacy policy — not through us.

Address lookup (reverse geocoding)

To show a readable address (not just raw coordinates) in the stamp, the app asks Android's built-in geocoding service to convert your GPS coordinates into an address. On most devices this request is handled by Google's geocoding backend via Google Play Services — meaning your coordinates are sent to Google for this one lookup, the same way they would be for any app that shows you a map address. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy, not by us — we don't see or store the result of this lookup beyond displaying it in the stamp.

Location services (Google Play Services)

The app uses Google Play Services' Fused Location Provider to obtain your GPS coordinates efficiently. This is a standard Android system component; its own data handling is covered by Google Play Services' privacy terms.

Analytics (Firebase Analytics)

The app uses Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used in aggregate — things like how often the app is opened, which screens are visited, and basic device/app information (device model, OS version, app version, country-level region, language). This is standard app- usage telemetry, collected automatically by the Firebase SDK; it does not include your photos, videos, GPS coordinates, or the address lookups described above — those stay governed entirely by the sections above this one.

Firebase Analytics is operated by Google and data collected through it is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase's own privacy terms. It assigns your device a randomly generated analytics identifier that is not tied to your name, email, or any account — because this app has no account system to tie it to in the first place.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect, and the app does not contain code to collect: your name, email, phone number, contacts, browsing history, or crash/diagnostic logs sent directly to us (outside of what's described in "Analytics" above). There is no account system, so there's nothing tied to a personal identity we hold.

Data retention and deletion

All photos, videos, and app settings live only on your device. Deleting a photo or video from your gallery, or uninstalling the app, removes everything the app created — we never receive a copy of any of it. Firebase Analytics data (see above) is retained by Google on Firebase's standard retention schedule; it isn't tied to a personal identity we hold, so there's no account-level deletion request to make — uninstalling the app stops any further collection.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from children. It has no age gate because it collects no data tied to a personal identity to begin with — everything described above either stays on-device, or (for the address lookup and Firebase Analytics) is a direct device-to-Google exchange the app triggers, not something routed through or stored by us.

Changes to this policy

If what the app does changes in a way that affects this policy, we'll update this page and change the effective date above.
